# Sales Performance Dashboard | Tableau BI Project

## Overview

This project is the second dashboard in my Revenue Intelligence BI Suite. It extends the original Revenue Intelligence Dashboard by focusing on rep-level revenue performance, leaderboard analysis, revenue concentration, and revenue distribution.

The goal of this project was to build a dashboard that answers a different layer of the business question: not just how revenue is performing overall, but which reps are driving it, how concentrated performance is across the sales team, and how evenly revenue is distributed.

## Relationship to the Original Project

This dashboard is a continuation of my original Revenue Intelligence Dashboard project.

The first dashboard focused on:
- won deals
- total revenue
- win rate
- average deal size
- monthly revenue trend
- product revenue performance

This second dashboard focuses on:
- rep-level won revenue
- top rep contribution
- average rep revenue
- leaderboard analysis
- revenue concentration
- distribution of rep performance

Together, the two dashboards form a small BI suite:
1. Executive revenue overview
2. Sales performance analysis

## Business Objective

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:
- Which sales reps are driving the most won revenue?
- Is performance concentrated in a small number of reps?
- How does each rep compare to the group average?
- Is sales performance broadly distributed or heavily skewed?

## Dataset

This dashboard uses rep-level revenue summary data exported from the broader Revenue Intelligence project workflow.

Key fields used:
- Sales Agent
- Won Revenue

## Dashboard Contents

The Sales Performance Dashboard includes:
- KPI card for total reps
- KPI card for top rep revenue
- KPI card for average rep revenue
- rep revenue leaderboard
- rep revenue treemap
- revenue distribution histogram

## Key Insights

A few patterns stood out from the analysis:
- revenue performance is uneven across the sales team
- a small number of reps contribute a large share of total won revenue
- one top-performing rep stands clearly above the rest
- the distribution of rep revenue suggests concentration rather than uniform performance
